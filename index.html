How to play

Use your hands to build the puzzle.

🤏
Frame the photo

Show two hands and pinch both index fingers to create the capture box.

⏳
Hold to capture

Keep the pinch steady for a moment and the photo will lock in.

🖐️
Drag puzzle pieces

Pinch any tile, move it into place, and release to drop it.

✊
Save the result

When the board is solved, make a fist to save the finished photo strip.
